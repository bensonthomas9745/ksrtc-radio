THE STORY

More than a commute. A sanctuary.

To anyone who grew up in Kerala or traveled along its serpentine highways, an ആനവണ്ടി (Aana Vandi) journey is never just public transportation. It is a shared emotional landmark.

It is the early morning chill sweeping through the iron grill; the rich aroma of damp soil after sudden tropical rain; the low, metallic thrum of the diesel engine; and the soothing music crackling softly through the cabin speaker above the driver's head.

"Sitting by that open shutter with cool wind on your face and a familiar melody playing quietly — there is no peace quite like it."

KSRTC Radio was created as an ambient shelter for non-resident Malayalis, students far from home, and lovers of nostalgia across the world. No signups, no ads, no distractions — just the journey.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is KSRTC Radio?
KSRTC Radio is a zero-dependency ambient web experience that simulates riding a Kerala State Road Transport Corporation bus. It combines looping passenger POV footage, 169+ shuffled evergreen Malayalam melodies, authentic engine rumbles, conductor bells, air horns, and monsoon rain mode.
What does "ആനവണ്ടി" (Aana Vandi) mean?
"ആനവണ്ടി" (Elephant Vehicle) is the beloved colloquial name given by generations of Malayalis to KSRTC buses, named after the twin royal elephants from the Travancore emblem on the corporation's seal.
Can I search or choose specific songs?
Yes. Click the "Songs" button on the top right navigation bar to slide open the playlist drawer. You can search in real time by movie, track title, or singer, and jump straight to any song.
Does it work on mobile browsers and Instagram?
Yes. It is fully responsive across mobile portrait and desktop widescreen views. When opened inside in-app browsers like Instagram or Facebook (which restrict background playback), an option is provided to open in Safari or Google Chrome.
Is KSRTC Radio free and official?
KSRTC Radio is 100% free and non-commercial. It is an independent cultural fan tribute to Kerala and its iconic public transit system. All logos, songs, and video assets belong to their respective copyright holders.
